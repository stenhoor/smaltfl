About this flow

NYC yellow-taxi trips from the TLC's records, one load a day as a feed would bring them, checked, joined to their zones and each borough's hourly weather, all run in this browser. Step through January 2024, or a real incident from the records. Each load is judged against what a day like it has, the same weekday in the six weeks before and the same day last year, a holiday or Election Day against its own day last year: a dot on a card means news. Tap any card for its story: the news first, then its rows, columns, what it dropped, odd rows and history, each with what you can do about it. A day with something wrong is paused, not published: it waits (‖), readers keeping the last day published, until the owner decides to publish it as is (⚑) or not at all (✕). A short day with a part missing can be filled from a good day near it, every filled row marked with the day it came from (◧), or published with only the part that arrived (◐). A change that is meant to stay can be accepted as the new level (a step mark): the days after it are judged from it, and those at the same level publish under it. A day the history can't judge pauses too, as every day does in the small build, which has no history.
