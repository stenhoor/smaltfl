%LEVELS%

Fixing the problem, step by step

Crimson is the problem, amber is what still needs you, green is settled, and smalt blue is only ever what you can press. Each step changes only what it has to, and each can be undone, one at a time.

%FIX%

Exploring the data

Ctrl K leads with what to do now, answers questions from the page’s data with the buttons to act on them, and finds any kind, dataset or column by a word; every dataset has Explore rows, with its columns and what each one means.

%EXPLORE%

Pointing shows what carries a link

Point at a neighbour, or tap what carries its link, and the datasets behind it light up; point at a dataset and the kinds it reaches light up.

%POINT%

Opening a kind, frame by frame

Application grows into its panel; its neighbours' datasets fly back into their squares as the tiles slide to the sides; the lines wait for the move to end, then draw themselves in and their words follow. Half a second; none for people who ask for less motion.

%FILM%

Ten times the size

A generated estate of 22 kinds and 212 datasets, drawn at nine tenths of full size on a 1440 × 900 window. Each area places the kinds that relate side by side, and the whole is arranged so the links between areas run straight through the gaps.

%BIG%

Colour and motion

Neutrals with a slight lean toward smalt blue; one action colour; two status colours, each with its own mark so colour is never the only signal. Every pair below meets WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes.

On a phone and a tablet
