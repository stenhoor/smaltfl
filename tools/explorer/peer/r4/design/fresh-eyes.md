## The verdict before changing anything

Neither screen fails a test outright. What I would question is the system:

- one fact (today against usual) is drawn three ways;
- one chart steps off the type scale;
- one mark (the cost ✕) has two colours, and one voice (smaltfl) has two marks;
- two link styles share a section;
- two kinds of tip share the same map.

So round 4 is seams: subtract the extra ways of saying the same thing, and leave one of each.
