## Does it read as one system?

On the journeys' hubs, yes.

- **One magenta cause; a mild day amber end to end.** Alarm colour covers 2.5% of a serious paused day (it was 12%)
  and 0.3% of a mild one.
- **One figure for "how far from usual",** dq's: one scale, the day the one coloured column.
- **One control and four words for every check:** Pause · Warn · Note · Off. The chosen segment is tonal (the gaps:
  5 chosen, 0 smalt rings). Larkspur's pick is a dot, and the dot is named.
- **One filled button a screen, and type on the scale** across the map, banner, day card, Decide and gaps.
- **Every decision screen is at 188 to 250 words,** with the map behind counted.
