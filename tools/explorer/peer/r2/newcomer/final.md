## What now works for a first-time viewer

- **The gaps step (1-setup/21 to 26) went from the worst screen to the best.** It is 476 words → 384, and every
  question it asks is answered on screen:
  - why it asks: "Accept each as known, or it is listed every day";
  - what the four words do, with "· Larkspur's pick" and "The 56 days: Feb 1 to Mar 27" said once;
  - the one gap worth asking about comes first;
  - each gap is a bar from zero, with what its setting does over the 56 days;
  - pointing at a gap lights its files on the map;
  - "Accept the 5 explained ›" counts what is on screen.
- **Decide (videos 2, 4a, 4b) leads with the answer.** It shows:
  - how serious it is;
  - the day's column against 28 grey days;
  - one line per choice, each with where readers will look and what follows ("Stays yours to decide" against
    "Reopens if a file comes");
  - her pick chosen;
  - "Goes on record when you tap Agree".

  Every decision screen is at 188 to 247 words, against 814 to 1,009 at the start.
- **A file opened shows a real row (3-explore/05).** Values sit beside their meanings, and outputs held by another file
  say so once. The rows open on the column you tapped.
- **Setup shows its steps:** a dashed box says which file names it, and the join is checked on screen with its values
  meeting.
