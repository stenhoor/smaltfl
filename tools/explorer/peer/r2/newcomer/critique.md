## Problems, worst first

### 1. Six decisions at once, with no why (1-setup/21 to 26)

- **What is wrong:**
  - "Problems already in the data on Feb 1 · 6", then a counting paragraph: "(1 warn if more appear, 1 noted, 3 off, 1
    watched by your rule). The finding is left for you to accept on its own."
  - Then six rows in data words, each with "Warn if more / Note it / Stop checking".
  - Nothing says why I must accept these, or what happens if I don't.
  - Nothing says what "Note it" does that "Warn if more" doesn't.
  - Nothing says what "the finding" is.
  - The button says "Accept the 5" while 6 are listed.
  - 913 to 942 visible words, the most of any step. The tool also counts the closed folds, which hold about 480 of them.
- **What I was doing, and why it matters:** I wanted to finish setup. Instead I was asked six questions in a language
  I hadn't learned yet. This is the owner's "skipping steps … I don't know why", now at the end of setup.
- **Principles:** §2 everything at the same volume, steps that happen off screen; §3.5 one focal point; §3.7 the answer
  first; §3.8 show the step; §7 clear hierarchy.
