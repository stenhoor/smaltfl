## What survived, from my lens

- **The join checked at its step** (1-setup/17 and 18): two columns, three values meeting their twins, "All 176 ids
  found on Feb 1's 2,401 rows", one tap off and on.
- **Unread kinds are grey,** and the map's line names its columns.
- **The gaps step** (21 to 25) is dq's combination with my pieces: pointing at a gap lights its files and its line
  (snapshot 23), one fold per row, and every setting's consequence on its row.
- **Both of my round-2b review fixes landed.**
  - The map's line stays lit through a pick: a `MutationObserver` re-lights it when labels redraw (`build.js`, about
    line 554).
  - The rules note no longer says "Pauses, Warns, Notes or Off".

## Best

**You see why each file joins what it joins, and what each gap's setting will do, before you tap.** The join card, the
one-scale gap bars with "Warns on 1 of 56 days: Feb 24", and the map lit where a gap lives.
