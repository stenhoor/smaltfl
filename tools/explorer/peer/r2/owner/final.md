## What now works best

**The decision itself.** Five minutes before a meeting I get my four answers at a glance, then one tap:

- **How serious:** a Serious or Mild badge with the pause mark, and dq's 28 days drawn from zero with the day's column
  and the labelled limit.
- **What readers see now and after each choice:** "Readers see Sun Feb 25 until you decide", then one eye line a
  choice, with what becomes of the day ("Stays yours to decide", "Reopens if a file comes").
- **Who recommends what:** "Recommended by Sloane Marsden, security lead · Mon Feb 26, 08:10", already chosen, with her
  words.
- **One tap, on record:** "Goes on record when you tap Agree: … Sloane Marsden's reason, agreed", then **Agree: wait
  for the file**.

Risks show where they should: the risky choice's always, the others' on hover and focus. Behind the panel the map keeps
only the subject: MFA, its line, "Held · 11".

Every decision step is 188 to 246 visible words with the map counted, against 814 to 1,009 at the start
(2-problem-fix/07 to 12 and 17 to 18; 4b-outlier/06 to 10; 4a-odd-but-ok/07 to 11). It works on a phone.

## The one problem most worth fixing next

**The record I sign is the hardest thing to read** (2-problem-fix/13, 14 and 19; 4b-outlier/11).

- **Each receipt is one run-on sentence.** For example: "Decided by Alex Kim, head of IT controls: published the
  corrected file · on Tue Feb 27 of the replay, at Nov 22, 23:07 · '…' Sloane Marsden's reason, agreed".
- **It mixes two clocks.** The recommendation is in the story's time ("Mon Feb 26, 08:10"). The decision is in the
  replay's wall time ("Nov 22, 23:06"). The wait and the publish then read one minute apart, and nine months after the
  day.
- **It is a step without its why.** It is the screen an auditor reads (2/19: 366 words).

The fix: each decision is a row of a short timeline:

- what (waited, published the corrected file);
- who;
- when, in the story's time, with the wall time on demand;
- whose words.

The day's own line goes above the rows: "Readers see Monday's own corrected file".
